Written by a human, for humans. Last updated: March, 2026

    I'm an immigrant. A hard worker from humble beginnings.

    I grew up in a small city in southeastern Europe, where I learned to appreciate what I have and be grateful for every opportunity I was given. Whether it was flipping burgers at a local joint when I was 16, or working for one of the largest game publishers in the world.

    I've been lucky enough to work alongside incredibly talented people and learn something from every single one of them. To live in San Francisco and watch some of the most amazing things in tech and science get built right in front of me. I don't take any of it for granted.

    I'm also a consumer. Of apps, games, tools, cool new gadgets. Whether it's a parking spot finder, a language learning app, a system that helps you navigate your taxes, or the social app I spend five hours a day mindlessly scrolling. I've seen it all.

    In the age of AI, with things moving as fast as they are, what we make ultimately remains unchanged. Vessels for entertainment, or tools that help you create something or solve a problem. But what matters most is the people. Part of my job is to keep that as the first and primary thing in my mind as we build something new. That applies to the people I work with and the people I build things for. People first.

    So what do I actually do? My path started in web design and graphic design. From there I moved into product design, and somewhere along the way I picked up code. That changed everything. It let me cross over into creative technology and technical art, a space where I could build tools, work on games, and bring ideas to life end to end. Right now, I work on AI pipelines, which feels like the natural next chapter for someone who's always lived between design and engineering.

    Thank you, truly, for taking the time to read.

    Nenad M.
